# Project browser: decisions

- Runs on real data. A recording's readings come from `get_project_activity` (`src-tauri/src/project_browser/activity.rs`): clicks, typing and picture readings (the editor's own readers, path-based), the transcript cache, moments with their note transcripts, and the saved timeline edit. The browser finds beats with the editor's `recordingBeats` and maps time with `recordingStripTime`, so a card's overview and the strip agree. Shortcuts are left out (the keyboard timeline is the editor's to work out).
- A card's steps are the editor's beats. A card scrubs the editor's scrub strip (24 frames along the edit); the caption at the pointer is the line said there, else the beat's name (`beatLabel`).
- Along the foot of its picture, inside its rounded corners, a recording's card draws the editor's own overview, `Minimap`, as a picture only (`isPicture`): a lull is the straight line and a beat a short bar, a moment's in its kind's colour, exactly as in the editor, with no view window, no squeeze or resize handles and nothing to press. It sits on a band of the badges' backing (`fill-strong`, blurred, in the appearance the picture's bottom corner calls for), so it reads on a light picture as on a dark one; the caption and length badges stand on that band, the same `inset-sm` above it as they stand above a picture's edge without one. A screenshot, or a recording not yet read, has no band, so every card is picture then name with the same spacing and no empty slot. It spans what plays now; what the edit took out is not drawn past its end. Its playhead (the pointer's scrub, or a find pointed at) and search finds are `primary`, never the brand orange, on the line only. The browser has no activity rail of its own (lulls drawn as springs): one look for the same recording in both places, and springs packed along a card read as noise.
- No peek: a press on a card opens it in the editor, as Return does on a focused card, and ⌘O / ⌘↓ (Control on Windows); the arrows go between cards. ⌘-click (Control-click on Windows) or the checkbox chooses a card and Shift-click takes a range, without opening it; in Recently Deleted, where nothing opens, a plain press chooses. Space does nothing among the cards. Choosing a search find opens its project. Opening starts the editor at its start: opening at a moment needs the editor to take a start position (not done).
- A card's ⋯ button is always shown, in every state, so its actions are found without pointing at the card first. The selection box still comes up only under the pointer or focus until something is chosen.
- Cards do not lift under the pointer; the scrub is their answer to pointing.
- Search covers names, what was said and moments (names and voice notes) within the source and kind filter; finds are a list (`ListBox`) beside the card turned to the first find.
- Search also reads when a project was recorded (`createdMs`, never opened or modified), in `search/date-query.ts` (no parsing library): today, yesterday, a weekday (its latest, today included), a month (its latest that has begun) or month and year, a day and month in either order with an optional year (its latest that has begun), a year up to this one, this or last week (starting on the locale's first day of the week) and month, and recent spans ("an hour ago", "last hour", "2 hours ago", "30 minutes ago"), each the span from that long before the query was typed up to now. An hour ("3pm", "3 pm", "15:00") is that hour; parts of the day are fixed: morning 5–12, afternoon 12–17, evening 17–21, night 21–5 the next morning. A time goes with a day ("monday 3pm", "yesterday morning"); alone it is its latest that has begun, so "3pm" read at 10am is yesterday's. A query that names two dates ("monday tuesday"), or a day the month lacks, is words. Weekday and month names, long and short, come from the format locale through `Intl` (case, accents and stops ignored; a name that is both, Spanish "mar", is neither); the other words are Fluent lists (`project-browser-date-words-…`, comma-separated), and the source's English words are understood beside a translation's, which also keeps them working in the pseudo-locales. The search runs in TypeScript, so the date filters there.
- A date alone lists its projects as the source lists them (grouped by date in a location) under how it was read ("Recorded yesterday", "Recorded Monday, Oct 27, 3 – 4 PM", "Recorded in the last hour"), formatted in the app locale; with words ("monday pricing") the words are searched within that date, as finds, under the same heading with the count beneath. Words that read as a date but are found as typed stay words ("may launch" finds the project named "May launch" whenever it was recorded), and a date alone that matches nothing falls back to the projects named with it ("may" finds it when nothing was recorded in May); otherwise it shows "No results", even where the word was said.
- Storage strip in the sidebar foot: one segment per listed project by size; pointing a segment outlines its card in `fg-tertiary` (an outline, not a lift, kept so the two stay linked), pointing a card lights its segment. Count and size are laid out apart; the middle-dot messages are gone.
- Pressing a segment goes to its card (`storage/use-go-to-card.ts`). The strip draws the open source's projects, so the card is always in the list open and the sidebar never switches source; the kind filter, the search, or both are lifted only when they hide it (the search is checked against what it would find once the kind is lifted). The card is scrolled to the middle of the pane (smoothly, instantly under reduced motion), clear of the edge fades and the island, given focus, and outlined as pointing outlines it for 1.5 s, since its focus ring shows only after the keyboard. A project whose drive is missing has no size, so it has no segment.
- The segments are a `Toolbar` of React Aria buttons, one Tab stop with the arrows between them (Home and End to the ends), each named "Go to {title}, {size}"; a listbox would announce a choice the strip does not hold. Focusing a segment lights it and its card as pointing does. Each is pressed across the 1 px gap after it and 4 px above and below what is drawn, so the smallest is 4 × 16 px to hit.
- Not in the browser: takes (the app has none), a pick-up shelf (nothing records where editing was left), "could be freed" (no export records), an editor step-in (the editor is its own window).
- Kept from the legacy browser: sources, locations and their menu, Add Location, Open File, kind filter, selection and the island (copy progress, undo delete, errors and open failures), rename, the project menu, Recently Deleted. The legacy browser had no drag and drop; none was added.
- Performance: the card the pointer is over lives in a small store (`storage/pointed-card.ts`) read only by the storage strip, so crossing cards no longer draws every card again; readings that land within a frame are applied together; the strip's segments measure their layout only when the projects change.
- The overview keeps time left to right in every language; the storage strip mirrors in right-to-left layouts (it is a part list, not a timeline).
- Dates: a card says when its project was recorded (made, for a screenshot), never when it was last edited: the weekday and time within the last six days (whose weekdays all differ), the day and month earlier this year, the full date before that (`recordedStyle`, formatted with `useDateFormatter`). The manifest keeps no recording time, so it is the project folder's creation time (`createdMs`), which the capture makes as it starts and which renaming and moving within a drive keep; a copy (a move to another drive, a duplicate) is given its source's on macOS, the one system that lets a folder's be set. The manifest's modified time (`modifiedMs`) is kept only to tell a cached picture or reading it is stale.
- Locations (the projects folder, added folders, drives) group and sort newest recorded first: Today, Yesterday, Previous 7 Days, then a group per month (the year named only for earlier years); opening or editing a project leaves it where it is. The rolling Previous 30 Days group is gone.
- Recent is "recently opened": the app's own list of recent projects (`project-library.json` in app data, not in projects), most recently opened first, ungrouped and without opened times. A project goes to its top when it opens in the editor (focusing one already open counts), and when a recording is made or kept or a screenshot taken.
- The cards fill the pane: as many columns as fit at 15rem each (`CARD_COLUMNS`), sharing what is left, so a card is at most a little wider than that unless it is alone; the loading placeholders use the same columns. Date groups with few projects still leave the rest of their row empty (`Folder/FullDay` shows a full row).
- In search results the card takes 13–20rem and the finds keep to a reading width (`max-w-xl`) beside it; a card's caption badge shrinks and is cut short beside the length badges instead of running under them.
- A card's selection box sits on a small blurred disc of `fill-strong` in the appearance the picture's corner calls for, as the badges do, so it reads on a busy picture; the `Checkbox` primitive is unchanged.
- A card's still (`preview.png`) and scrub strip (`scrub.jpg`) are derived pictures the open editor composes (`editor/project_thumbnail`): its look comes from the editor's resolved settings, so the browser cannot recompose them on its own. They carry no version: when the compositing changes (as when a camera cut out of its background began casting its shadow from the person rather than its frame), an old project's pictures are made again the next time it is opened in the editor, which always composes them on opening; until then the browser shows the old ones. Composing them rewrites only those two files, never the project.
- The island shrinks away to nothing, as the editor islands do (`shrinksToNothing`), rather than to the speck the recording island leaves without a notch, which read as a pill left over the projects; it comes out of nothing the same way.
- The island stays out while what it holds changes, and changes as the editor's island does: the row (the choice, a copy under way, a failure, a delete with its Undo) and the line under a choice clear into the new one in place (`IslandSwap`, keyed by what it says, so a new failure or a second delete clears in too) while the black glides to the new size. The row spans the island (`rowFillsWidth`), the count at its start and the actions at its end, so they keep to the black's edges as it glides; Select all and the choice's size come and go with `IslandRowPresence`, and the count rolls. It used to cut one row for the next while the black glided, with nothing blurred or revealed.
- Why a project would not open is said in a short translated sentence that names what is wrong (`OpenFailure` in `editor/project_open`, from the project crate's `ReadError`), never a path; the details, path included, go to the log. The island's status wraps inside its width, a word too long for it included, its words ending as far from the island's end as its glyph stands from the start.
- An empty list says only what is so ("No projects", "No recently deleted projects", "No results"), with Open File in Recent; no sentence explaining it.
- What a search found is marked in the warning tint wherever it shows, as the editor's transcript marks it (`found-text`): in the finds, the cards' names and the caption on a card's picture. A name that can be renamed in place is marked with a highlight over the field (`useFoundHighlight`, `::highlight(found-text)`), so editing it is undisturbed.
