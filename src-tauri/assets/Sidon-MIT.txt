Screenwide downloads the Sidon v0.1 speech restoration model for Studio sound, as
the ONNX conversion published at
https://github.com/barrett-snapshot/voice-isolation-models. Its notice follows.

voice-isolation-models — third-party notices

These files are derived from two MIT-licensed works. Changes: the released TorchScript graphs were
converted to ONNX (opset 17), and in sidon_fe.onnx Sidon's LoRA weights are merged into the
w2v-BERT 2.0 encoder. No weights were retrained.
